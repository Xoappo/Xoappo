Hello 👋,

My name is **Alexander (maybe Xoapp)**, a developer focused on **backend and web development**. I mainly work with **PHP** and **Java**, building applications with **Laravel**, and I'm getting my first hands-on experience with **Spring** in the JVM ecosystem.

My experience also covers data design and management: I'm comfortable with **MySQL**, **PostgreSQL**, **MariaDB**, **MongoDB** and **Redis**, choosing the right tool for each project's needs, from relational models to in-memory caching.

On the infrastructure side, I use **Docker** and **Kubernetes** to package, orchestrate and deploy services in a reproducible and scalable way.

I'm currently expanding my horizons with **Zig**, for its low-level control approach, and with **Kotlin** for **Android app development** — two technologies I'm actively learning.

I'd describe myself as curious and pragmatic: I like understanding the reasoning behind technical decisions, writing maintainable code and keeping learning with every project. I'm open to collaborating, contributing to development teams and growing alongside the community.

Thank you for taking the time to read my introduction.
